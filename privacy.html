Privacy Policy

Case Manager — Techno Juris Law Firm · Effective 22 October 2026

Case Manager is an internal tool used only by Techno Juris Law Firm for its own cases. It is not offered to anyone else. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

Google data the app uses

  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events): the app creates and updates one event per case for the next hearing date in the firm's own calendar, reads those events back to avoid duplicates, and removes duplicate hearing events it finds.
  • Google Drive files created by the app (drive.file): the app uploads each case's latest court order PDF to a folder in the firm's own Drive and attaches it to the hearing event. It cannot see or change any other file in the Drive.

Where data is kept

Case details and the Google sign-in token are stored only on the firm's own computer. Calendar events and order PDFs stay in the firm's own Google account.

Sharing

Google account data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train any model, and no person outside the firm can access it through this app.

Limited Use

Case Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events and files the app created stay in the account until deleted there.
