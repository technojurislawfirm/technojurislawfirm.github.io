Case Manager

Internal case-diary tool of Techno Juris Law Firm

Case Manager is a private tool used only by Techno Juris Law Firm to keep track of its own court cases. It reads public case status and cause lists from the official eCourts services, and puts each case's next hearing into the firm's own Google Calendar, with the latest court order attached from the firm's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts other than the firm's.
